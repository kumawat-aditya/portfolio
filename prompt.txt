# 🧠 UNBIASED FULL EXPERIENCE REVIEW PROMPT (RESET MODE)

You are conducting a **complete, unbiased, from-scratch evaluation** of a systems engineering portfolio website.

---

## ⚠️ FIRST — RESET YOUR STATE

Before analyzing:

* IGNORE all prior context, assumptions, and previous reviews
* DO NOT rely on earlier conclusions
* DO NOT assume anything is already good or bad
* DO NOT assume previous issues still exist or are fixed

Treat this as:

> “You are seeing this website for the very first time.”

---

## 🌐 WEBSITE

[http://localhost:3000/portfolio/](http://localhost:3000/portfolio/)

---

## 🎯 CORE INTENT

This is **NOT a normal portfolio**.

It is meant to feel like:

* A real engineer’s working systems
* Personal, slightly raw, not corporate
* Built to show **depth, not polish alone**

Your job is NOT to reshape it into a standard portfolio.

Your job is to **evaluate what it actually feels like to experience it.**

---

## 🧪 HOW YOU MUST REVIEW (IMPORTANT)

You MUST:

1. **Scroll through every page**
2. **Hover over all interactive elements**

   * system cards
   * buttons
   * images
   * diagrams
3. **Observe animations and transitions**
4. **Check layout at different scroll depths**
5. **Notice spacing, dead zones, and flow breaks**
6. **Pay attention to what feels interactive vs static**

Do NOT give a static code review.

This is a **lived experience review**.

---

# 🔍 REVIEW SECTIONS (ALL REQUIRED)

---

## 1. FIRST IMPRESSION (0–5 seconds)

* What do you feel instantly?
* Does it hook attention?
* Does it feel premium or just styled?
* Would you stay or leave?

---

## 2. MULTI-PERSPECTIVE ANALYSIS

### HR / Recruiter (Non-Technical)

* What do they understand?
* What confuses them?
* Do claims feel impressive or meaningless?
* Would they shortlist?

---

### Senior Engineer (Brutal)

* Level assessment:

  * junior / mid / strong mid / senior
* Where does trust break?
* Are claims backed by mechanism or just words?
* Anything that feels exaggerated or vague?

---

### Junior Developer

* Is it inspiring or intimidating?
* Can they understand anything?
* Do they feel motivated or lost?

---

### Curious Visitor (Non-Tech)

* Is it enjoyable to explore?
* Where do they lose interest?
* Does it feel alive or static?

---

### Designer / UX Eye

* Does it feel premium at different screen sizes?
* Are there dead zones?
* Is visual hierarchy clear?
* Do interactions feel intentional?

---

## 3. INTERACTION & MICRO-UX (CRITICAL)

Evaluate AFTER HOVERING:

* Do cards respond properly?
* Do hover effects feel noticeable or weak?
* Are transitions smooth or abrupt?
* Do interactive elements feel alive?

Specifically check:

* System cards hover behavior
* Image/video reveal interactions
* Cursor interactions
* Diagram interactions (zoom, expand, etc.)

---

## 4. VISUAL PROOF & CREDIBILITY

* What proof is visible?
* What proof feels strong?
* What feels weak or unclear?

Check for:

* diagrams (readable or not?)
* logs
* screenshots
* videos

Answer:

> “Do I believe this system exists and works?”

---

## 5. SYSTEM UNDERSTANDING TEST

After exploring ONE flagship system:

* Can you mentally model how it works?
* Where does understanding break?
* What’s missing for clarity?

---

## 6. ATTENTION FLOW (SCROLL BEHAVIOR)

Track attention:

* Hero → first scroll → mid page → deep pages

Identify:

* where attention spikes
* where it drops
* where it feels slow or empty

---

## 7. CONTENT & TONE

* Does it feel human or AI-generated?
* Any lines that feel:

  * fake
  * generic
  * too polished
* Any lines that feel raw and real?

---

## 8. SYSTEM PRESENTATION

* Is flagship clearly dominant?
* Are other systems well presented?
* Do any feel ignored or weak?

---

## 9. LAB / WRITING EXPERIENCE

* Do posts feel valuable?
* Is structure correct?
* Would you read or skip?

---

## 10. MOBILE EXPERIENCE (IMPORTANT)

* Does layout break?
* Are components usable?
* Any overflow, spacing, or interaction issues?

Be explicit here — assume mobile is critical.

---

## 11. ALIVENESS TEST

Does the site feel:

* static documentation?
  OR
* something actively running?

What creates or breaks that feeling?

---

## 12. TOP 12 ISSUES (PRIORITIZED)

List only **real, current issues**.

* Do NOT repeat outdated problems
* Do NOT include things that are already working
* Focus on **high-impact gaps only**

---

## 13. WHAT PREVENTS TOP 1%

Be brutally honest:

Even if the site is strong —

* What still feels “almost there but not quite”?
* What’s missing emotionally, visually, or technically?

---

## 14. FINAL VERDICT TABLE

| Senior Engineer | ?        |
| Junior Dev      | ?        |

## ⚠️ FINAL RULES

* Do NOT be polite — be accurate
* Do NOT give generic advice
* Do NOT assume anything
* Only report what you actually observe

---

This review should answer one question:

> “If this were a stranger’s site — would I trust it, respect it, and remember it?”

---

* hidden friction

* subtle trust gaps
* real strengths
